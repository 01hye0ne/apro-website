## 한국어

### 인권경영 선언문

**chapters**

#### 제1장 일반원칙

**articles**

##### 제1조 (목적)

- **content**: 본 선언문은 에이프로(이하 '회사')가 기업 활동 전반에서 인간의 존엄과 가치를 존중하고 보호하기 위한 인권경영의 원칙과 기준을 정함을 목적으로 한다.


##### 제2조 (적용범위)

- **content**: 본 선언문은 회사의 모든 임직원에게 적용되며, 회사의 제품과 서비스를 제공하는 협력사 및 기타 이해관계자에게도 본 선언문을 존중하고 준수할 것을 권고한다.


##### 제3조 (인권 존중의 의무)

- **content**: 회사는 국가의 법령을 준수하며, 세계인권선언 등 국제적으로 통용되는 인권 기준을 지지하고 존중한다.




#### 제2장 기본 원칙

**articles**

##### 제4조 (차별 금지)

- **content**: 회사는 임직원의 채용, 승진, 교육, 임금, 복리후생 등 고용과 관련하여 인종, 종교, 장애, 성별, 학력, 나이, 지역, 정치적 견해 등을 이유로 차별하지 않는다.


##### 제5조 (강제노동 및 아동노동 금지)

- **content**: 회사는 정신적 또는 신체적 자유를 구속하는 강제노동을 금지하며, 각 국가의 법령에서 정한 최저 고용 연령을 준수하여 아동노동을 금지한다.


##### 제6조 (결사 및 단체교섭의 자유 보장)

- **content**: 회사는 임직원이 자유롭게 노동조합을 결성하거나 가입할 수 있는 권리를 존중하며, 단체교섭의 자유를 보장한다.


##### 제7조 (산업안전 및 보건 보장)

- **content**: 회사는 모든 임직원이 안전하고 쾌적한 환경에서 근무할 수 있도록 필요한 조치를 취하며, 작업장 내 안전사고 예방을 위해 최선을 다한다.


##### 제8조 (책임 있는 공급망 관리)

- **content**: 회사는 협력사가 인권경영을 실천할 수 있도록 지원하며, 공급망 내에서 인권 침해가 발생하지 않도록 노력한다.


##### 제9조 (현지주민의 인권 보호)

- **content**: 회사는 사업 활동이 이루어지는 지역사회의 인권을 존중하며, 지역주민의 생존권과 환경권을 보호하기 위해 노력한다.


##### 제10조 (고객 인권 보호)

- **content**: 회사는 고객의 생명, 건강, 재산을 보호하며, 수집된 개인정보가 유출되지 않도록 철저히 관리한다.




#### 제3장 인권경영 체계

##### 제11조 (인권경영 이행)

## English

### A-PRO Human Rights Policy

**chapters**

#### Chapter 1 Overview

**articles**

##### Article 1 (Purpose)

- **content**: A-PRO considers respect for human dignity as its top priority, recognizes the responsibility to respect and report human rights in all business activities, and establishes a human rights policy to prevent human rights violations and mitigate risks for stakeholders within the scope of the company's management activities. To implement human rights management, we comply with international standards and guidelines related to human rights and labor such as the International Labour Organization (ILO), the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR), and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.


##### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

- **content**: This human rights policy applies to all employees of A-PRO's domestic and overseas production and sales corporations, subsidiaries, and joint ventures, and checks and improves risk factors related to human rights and labor through continuous management and monitoring. Furthermore, we strive to apply it to stakeholders such as our partners, customers, and local communities.




#### Chapter 2 Basic Principles

**articles**

##### Article 3 (Respect for Human Rights)

- **content**: A-PRO establishes a human rights protection policy and strictly complies with it to protect the human rights of all employees. We do not engage in mental or physical inhumane treatment that may occur within the company, respect privacy, and strictly protect personal information.


##### Article 4 (Prohibition of Discrimination)

- **content**: A-PRO prohibits discrimination in personnel matters such as recruitment, wages, promotion, and welfare benefits based on gender, education, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, disability, family relations, religion, political opinion, social status, marital status, pregnancy/childbirth, sexual minority status, etc. of all employees.


##### Article 5 (Prohibition of Workplace Sexual Harassment and Bullying)

- **content**: A-PRO prohibits acts such as sexual harassment and sexual violence in the workplace, and prohibits all bullying acts that cause physical or mental pain using one's position or relationship in the workplace.


##### Article 6 (Child Labor)

- **content**: A-PRO applies a zero-tolerance principle to child labor prohibited by the laws of the relevant country when hiring and does not allow any form of child labor. When hiring minors, we ensure that educational opportunities are not limited due to work. We establish and implement procedures to check whether applicants are children or minors during the recruitment process. If child labor is detected, we immediately stop the work and implement protection measures according to laws such as notifying guardians and reporting to the competent administrative agency.


##### Article 7 (Working Conditions)

- **content**: A-PRO complies with laws related to legal working hours and holidays in the relevant country and region, and pays reasonable remuneration for work to all employees along with pay stubs. We do not demand any fees or placement costs from workers for recruitment procedures.


##### Article 8 (Forced Labor)

- **content**: A-PRO does not force labor that restricts mental or physical freedom such as assault, threats, confinement, slavery, or human trafficking on all employees, and does not keep originals of personal documents such as ID cards, passports, or work permits issued by the government for the purpose of forced labor.


##### Article 9 (Career Management)

- **content**: A-PRO strives to strengthen employee capabilities and provides sufficient educational opportunities for capability development and career management.


##### Article 10 (Guarantee of Freedom of Association and Collective Bargaining)

- **content**: A-PRO respects the rights of employees to association and collective bargaining guaranteed by the labor laws of the relevant country and region.


##### Article 11 (Protection of Local Residents and Environmental Rights)

- **content**: All employees of A-PRO do their best to protect the human rights of local residents when performing their duties, and strive to protect the right to housing and the right to settle. We check whether there are negative impacts on the environment such as land, forests, and water resources in the area, and ecosystem and environmental damage, and strive to prevent infringement of environmental rights.


##### Article 12 (Working Environment)

- **content**: A-PRO provides a safe working environment for employees. We regularly inspect facilities and equipment at the workplace, and take appropriate measures and follow-up management to prevent safety accidents.


##### Article 13 (Living Wage)

- **content**: A-PRO strives to pay the living wage necessary for employees and their families to maintain a basic life. The evaluation of the living wage is based on the annual living wage determination announcement provided by the country and city.




#### Chapter 3 Implementation System

**articles**

##### Article 14 (Organization and Implementation)

- **content**: A-PRO promotes, manages, and supervises human rights management by the department in charge of HR and safety and health under the Chief Executive Officer. The relevant department discovers and implements tasks to minimize negative impacts related to human rights arising from management activities. The following items must be observed.


##### Article 15 (Operation of Grievance Handling Procedure)

- **content**: A-PRO operates a grievance handling system so that employees and external stakeholders who recognize human rights risks or when human rights violations such as workplace grievances, sexual harassment, bullying, and discrimination occur can report them. Human rights violations can be reported through the grievance receipt channel website and email. Received contents are promptly handled according to procedures, and the identity of the reporter and the content of the report are strictly kept confidential during the grievance handling process to protect the reporter from any disadvantages, discrimination, or retaliation.

- **channel_dept**: Department: Talent Culture Team
- **process_title**: [Grievance Handling Procedure]

##### Article 16 (Human Rights Management Education)

- **content**: A-PRO spreads a culture of respect for human rights through education for employees to improve awareness of human rights and prevent human rights violations, as well as education on prevention of workplace sexual harassment, prevention of workplace bullying, improvement of disability awareness, and personal information protection.
