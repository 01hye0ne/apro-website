APRO Co., Ltd. (“Party A”), pursuant to Article 527-3 of the Commercial Act, resolved to merge with APRO Partners Co., Ltd. (“Party B-1”) and Ecovolt Co., Ltd. (“Party B-2”) via a resolution of the Board of Directors on October 28, 2024, without the approval procedures of a General Meeting of Shareholders. “Party B-1” and “Party B-2” resolved to merge via resolutions of their respective Extraordinary General Meetings of Shareholders on the same date.

As it has been resolved that “Party A” shall succeed to all rights and obligations of “Party B-1” and “Party B-2” by absorbing them, and that “Party B-1” and “Party B-2” shall be dissolved, we hereby give public notice that any creditors with objections to this merger are requested to submit their objections to the respective companies within one month from the day following the publication date of this notice.

Furthermore, since “Party A” owns 100% of the issued shares of “Party B-1” and “Party B-2,” no new shares will be allocated/issued in connection with this merger. Shareholders holding shares of “Party B-1” or “Party B-2” are requested to submit their share certificates to the respective companies within one month from the day following the publication date of this notice.
