## 한국어

### 협력사 행동 규범

**sections**

#### 1. 개요

- **content**: 에이프로는 모든 협력사가 비즈니스 운영에 있어 높은 윤리적 기준을 유지하고, 법규를 준수하며, 사회적 책임을 다할 것을 기대합니다. 본 행동규범은 에이프로와 거래하는 모든 협력사가 준수해야 할 최소한의 기준을 제시합니다.


#### 2. 노동 및 인권

- **items**
  - 자발적 근로: 모든 근로는 자발적이어야 하며, 강제노동, 노예노동 또는 인신매매를 금지합니다.
  - 아동노동 금지: 각 국가의 법령에서 정한 최저 고용 연령을 준수하며, 아동노동을 엄격히 금지합니다.
  - 근로시간: 해당 국가의 법정 근로시간 및 휴일 규정을 준수합니다.
  - 임금 및 복리후생: 최저임금, 초과근무 수당 등 법정 급여 기준을 준수하고, 법으로 정해진 복리후생을 제공합니다.
  - 차별 금지 및 인도적 대우: 인종, 종교, 성별 등을 이유로 한 차별을 금지하며, 가혹행위나 성희롱 등 비인도적인 대우를 금지합니다.


#### 3. 안전 및 보건

- **items**
  - 산업안전: 작업장 내 위험 요소를 식별하고 관리하여 안전한 작업 환경을 제공합니다.
  - 비상사태 대비: 화재 등 비상 상황에 대비한 대응 절차를 마련하고 훈련을 실시합니다.
  - 산업재해 및 질병: 산업재해 및 질병을 예방하기 위한 체계를 구축하고 관리합니다.


#### 4. 환경

- **items**
  - 환경 법규 준수: 환경 관련 법규 및 인허가 사항을 철저히 준수합니다.
  - 오염 방지 및 자원 절약: 폐기물, 폐수, 대기오염 물질 배출을 최소화하고 자원을 효율적으로 사용합니다.
  - 유해물질 관리: 인체나 환경에 유해한 화학물질을 안전하게 관리하고 배출을 통제합니다.


#### 5. 윤리 및 공정거래

- **items**
  - 비즈니스 청렴성: 뇌물, 부패, 강요, 횡령 등 모든 형태의 부정부패를 금지합니다.
  - 부당이득 금지: 뇌물이나 기타 부당한 이득을 제공하거나 받는 행위를 금지합니다.
  - 정보 공개: 비즈니스 활동, 재무 상태 등에 관한 정보를 관련 법규에 따라 투명하게 공개합니다.
  - 지식재산권 보호: 에이프로 및 제3자의 지식재산권을 존중하고 보호합니다.
  - 공정거래 및 광고: 공정거래 관련 법규를 준수하고, 허위 광고를 지양합니다.


#### 6. 경영 시스템

- **items**
  - 행동규범 준수 선언: 본 행동규범의 내용을 이해하고 준수할 것을 약속합니다.
  - 리스크 관리: 비즈니스 운영과 관련된 리스크를 식별하고 관리하는 프로세스를 운영합니다.
  - 시정 조치: 본 행동규범 위반 사항이 발견될 경우 즉시 시정 조치를 취합니다.

## English

### A-PRO Supplier Code of Conduct

**sections**

#### Article 1 (Overview)

- **content**: As a leading company in the global secondary battery industry, A-PRO intends to implement responsible management in terms of environment, society, and governance. We are doing our best to minimize negative environmental and social impacts caused by management activities and expand positive impacts. This Code of Conduct was enacted reflecting global guidelines, laws, and systems, and we expect APRO and its suppliers to implement responsible management activities and grow together by complying with this Code of Conduct through mutual efforts. APRO will strive to apply international standard codes of conduct such as the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, the UN Global Compact (UNGC) 10 Principles, and the International Labor Organization (ILO) Core Conventions, centering on the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights, to a sustainable supply chain.


#### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

- **content**: It applies to suppliers (raw material suppliers, facility/construction companies, consignees, etc.) who have signed a contract for transaction. Suppliers subject to application should recommend that the entire supply chain, including business partners (sub-suppliers), comply with the matters presented in this Code of Conduct.


#### Article 3 (Responsibilities and Roles of Suppliers)

- **content**: Suppliers must consider compliance with this Code of Conduct in the process of management decision-making and business operation. APRO and third-party organizations entrusted by APRO may inspect or conduct due diligence on suppliers in accordance with domestic and foreign laws and agreements regarding whether suppliers are complying with the matters presented in this Code of Conduct. APRO may recommend improvement for risks identified based on the results of such inspection or due diligence, and suppliers must establish and implement a risk mitigation plan through consultation with APRO. Suppliers must understand and be aware that compliance with this Code of Conduct may be a major criterion for supplier selection, and furthermore, if they do not make appropriate efforts for improvement even after violating this Code of Conduct, changes may occur in the transaction relationship with APRO. This Code of Conduct is not a document specifying all compliance matters of suppliers, and this Code of Conduct may be regularly supplemented and revised for the establishment of a sustainable supply chain.


#### Article 4 (Protection and Respect for Human Rights)

- **items**
  - Suppliers do not discriminate in employment, promotion, education, welfare benefits, etc. on the grounds of gender, nationality, age, education, religion, region, disability, gender identity, marriage and pregnancy status, political orientation, social status, etc.
  - Suppliers make every effort for prevention, such as regularly conducting education for all employees to improve awareness so that cases of workplace bullying including sexual harassment do not occur, and if cases of workplace bullying occur, they apply a zero-tolerance principle and implement immediate measures.
  - Suppliers must not discriminate against employees in the payment of wages and the operation of welfare benefit systems.
  - Suppliers must protect women's human rights and support gender equality, and establish maternity protection policies and procedures regarding working conditions, etc. in accordance with local laws to protect the health and safety of pregnant and breastfeeding female employees. Maternity protection subjects must not be treated disadvantageously in work placement, wages, etc.
  - Suppliers must provide a pleasant working environment for employees and strive to operate a welfare benefit system to improve the quality of life.
  - Suppliers must comply with the legal working hours of each country where they operate business, and manage working hours including rest.
  - Suppliers must respect the privacy of employees and refrain from unnecessary work instructions outside of working hours.
  - Suppliers must not have inhumane treatment including sexual harassment, sexual abuse, corporal punishment, mental or physical coercion, and abusive language towards employees, and there must be no threat to do such treatment.
  - Workplace bullying that causes physical or mental pain or worsens the working environment beyond the appropriate scope of work by using the superiority of status or relationship in the workplace between employees must be prohibited, and the privacy of employees must be respected and unnecessary work instructions outside of working hours must be avoided.
  - Recognize and respect workers' rights to freedom of association and collective bargaining.
  - Do not use child labor in any form, and suppliers must comply with the following. 'Child' means a person under the age of 15, the age at which compulsory education ends, or the highest age among the legal minimum employment ages under local laws. If child workers are found, the supplier must take immediate response measures.


#### Article 5 (Safety and Health)

- **items**
  - Provide a working environment that meets local and national safety, health, and fire safety laws.
  - Take all measures including legal requirements such as risk assessment, continuous education and emergency response training, and provision of personal protective equipment so that employees can work in a safe and healthy working environment.
  - Perform regular risk assessments and implement corrective and preventive measures to minimize workplace hazards, including mechanical, electrical, chemical, fire, and physical hazards.
  - All workplaces must have an effective fire safety management system and emergency plan.
  - Strive to keep all facilities clean and provide them to employees.
  - Physical labor risks must be controlled in advance, and safety risks of production facilities or other facilities must be checked.
  - Suppliers must regularly conduct general health checkups or special health checkups for employees in accordance with the health checkup laws of each country where they operate business. In addition, if necessary as a result of the health checkup, measures such as changing the employee's workspace, changing work, or shortening working hours must be implemented.


#### Article 6 (Environment)

- **items**
  - Suppliers must comply with environmental laws and regulations of each country where they operate business, and acquire and maintain all environmental permits required for business operation.
  - Suppliers must strive to ensure that chemical substances handled in the process of business operation are safely managed during transportation, storage, use, and disposal. In addition, information that can identify the risk and hazard of handled chemical substances must be marked or disclosed.
  - Suppliers must strive to check whether raw materials and parts procured, produced, sold, and distributed contain substances harmful to the human body or the environment.
  - Suppliers must establish a system to measure energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions and strive to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
  - Suppliers must identify the characteristics of generated waste, dispose of it after treatment in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, and strive to reduce the amount generated.


#### Article 7 (Ethical Management)

- **items**
  - Suppliers conduct business with high ethical standards as a business without bribery and corruption, honestly and responsibly according to established work regulations.
  - Suppliers comply with fair trade-related laws and standards of each country where they operate business, prohibit unfair competition acts, and maintain market fairness.
  - A zero-tolerance policy that does not allow any form of bribery, corruption, unfair profits, and embezzlement must be used.
  - All transactions must be made transparently and accurately reflected in accounting books and business records.
  - Suppliers must respect the intellectual property rights of all transaction partners in the supply chain including our company, and strive for mutual information protection such as trade secrets, internal information, and technology/know-how.
  - Suppliers comply with export regulations and economic sanctions laws of each country, and do not trade with countries, regions, corporations, organizations, individuals, etc. falling under this.
  - Suppliers must operate a grievance handling system where employees can report violations of relevant internal regulations and laws such as ethics, environment, labor, human rights, safety and health, and information security. In addition, all measures and efforts must be made so that reporters and whistleblowers are not subject to unreasonable measures due to reporting/whistleblowing through protection.
  - Suppliers must truthfully disclose information on labor/safety and health/environment management status, management activities, governance structure, financial status, and performance in accordance with relevant laws and industry best practices.


#### Article 8 (Prohibition of Use of Conflict Minerals)

- **content**: Suppliers must establish a policy not to use raw materials obtained through illegal and unethical methods (e.g., minerals mined in mines occupied by armed groups, timber logged in forest conservation and logging prohibited areas, etc.).


#### Article 9 (Management System)

- **items**
  - Suppliers must comply with the Code of Conduct, laws related to corporate operations and products, and customer requirements, and establish a management system that considers workers' human rights, safety, health, environment, and corporate ethics when making corporate management decisions.
  - Goals, targets, and implementation plans for performance improvement in social, environmental, and health and safety, and performance against goals must be periodically evaluated and monitored.
  - Suppliers must make reasonable efforts to protect the personal information of everyone involved in the business, including suppliers, customers, consumers, and employees. In addition, they must comply with laws related to personal information protection and information security in the collection, storage, processing, transmission, and sharing of personal information.
  - Suppliers must comply with environmental laws and regulations of each country where they operate business, and acquire and maintain all environmental permits required for business operation.
  - Suppliers must strive to ensure that chemical substances handled in the process of business operation are safely managed during transportation, storage, use, and disposal. In addition, information that can identify the risk and hazard of handled chemical substances must be marked or disclosed.
