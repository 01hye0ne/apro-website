## English

### Environmental Management Policy

**sections**

#### Article 1 (Purpose)

- **content**: A-PRO establishes this policy to achieve environmental conservation and corporate sustainable management simultaneously by minimizing environmental impact and sustainable use of resources. This is to faithfully implement domestic and foreign environmental laws and ESG standards and realize responsible management that meets the expectations of customers and stakeholders.


#### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

- **content**: This policy applies to all A-PRO business sites and all suppliers, and is implemented together with internal regulations related to environmental management. A-PRO complies with domestic and foreign environmental laws and regulations in preference to this policy, and performs work in accordance with this environmental management policy for matters not specifically covered by relevant laws or without explicit standards.


#### Article 3 (Environmental Management Policy)

- **items**
  - Create long-term corporate value and fulfill social responsibilities through environmental management.
  - Continuously promote concrete implementation plans such as introducing renewable energy and improving energy efficiency to achieve greenhouse gas reduction.
  - Maintain the discharge of pollutants such as air, water quality, and soil below legal standards and actively strive for pollutant reduction activities.
  - Consider resource saving and energy efficiency improvement from the product design stage to the entire production process.
  - Prevent ecosystem damage caused by corporate activities in advance and strive to minimize biodiversity loss.
  - Suppress waste generation, expand recycling, and strengthen the pre-identification and management system for hazardous chemical substances.
  - Systematically manage environmental management performance and realize responsible environmental management through continuous improvement.
  - Manage water usage for sustainable use of water resources and strengthen water saving and reuse efforts.


#### Article 4 (Responsibilities and Authority)

- **intro**: Responsibilities and authorities related to the environmental management policy are as follows.
- **items**
  - The Infrastructure Team is responsible for overall environmental management.
  - The Infrastructure Team cooperates with relevant departments to perform policy establishment, performance management, education, and external response.
  - The CEO oversees the establishment and execution of environmental management strategies and has decision-making authority on major environmental issues.
  - The executing department implements environmental management activities in its field, regularly reports performance data to the Infrastructure Team, and performs cause analysis and corrective actions when problems occur.


#### Article 5 (Supplier Environmental Management)

- **content**: When signing a contract with a supplier, A-PRO requires compliance with environmental laws and practice of eco-friendly management, and reflects this in the supplier evaluation and management criteria. In addition, we provide relevant education and technical support if necessary so that suppliers can faithfully fulfill environmental requirements.


#### Article 6 (Stakeholder Communication)

- **content**: A-PRO strengthens communication with stakeholders such as customers and suppliers, and transparently shares major environmental performance and improvement efforts. In addition, we promote awareness of environmental management and spread practice through regular education and internal communication for employees.


#### Article 7 (Environmental Management Policy)

**items**

##### Greenhouse Gas Emissions

- **content**: Continuously promote concrete implementation plans such as introducing renewable energy and improving energy efficiency to achieve greenhouse gas reduction. Consider energy efficiency improvement from the product design stage to the entire production process. Regularly monitor workplace greenhouse gas emissions, establish emission targets, and improve/manage them.


##### Water Resource Management

- **content**: Manage efficiently by reducing water usage and increasing reuse and recycling for sustainable use of water resources. Regularly monitor water usage, establish usage targets, and improve/manage them.


##### Pollutant Management

- **content**: Measure and manage the amount of pollutants generated if pollutants such as air, water quality, and soil occur. Check the performance and operation efficiency of prevention facilities and establish improvement plans. Maintain pollutant discharge below legal standards and actively strive for pollutant reduction activities.


##### Waste Management

- **content**: Maximize waste suppression and expand recycling to achieve waste generation reduction. Regularly monitor waste generation, establish usage targets, and improve/manage them.


##### Chemical Substance Management

- **content**: Implement necessary measures to prevent harm to workers' health and the environment caused by chemical substances. Periodically check the MSDS of chemical substances, update to the latest version, and conduct MSDS education for workers handling them.


##### Biodiversity Management

- **content**: Predict negative impacts on biodiversity in advance and implement measures to minimize them. Strive to perform biodiversity conservation activities considering the opinions of stakeholders.




#### Article 8 (Enforcement Date)

- **content**: This policy will be enforced from June 1, 2025, and will be regularly reviewed and revised reflecting changes in relevant laws and ESG standards, and customer requirements.
