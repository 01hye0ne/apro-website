## Environment / Safety & Health

### Maximizing Energy Efficiency

- **desc**: Value of minimizing energy waste and reducing carbon emissions through advanced technology
- **tasks**
  - Advancing environmental management system
  - Establishing basis for climate change response
  - Developing energy efficiency-centered technology
  - Creating sustainable production environment
- **main_title**: Green Future with Eco-Friendly Technology
- **main_desc**: A-PRO takes climate change response and environmental protection as core corporate values. We maximize energy efficiency through innovative power conversion technology and practice eco-friendly management for a sustainable planet.
- **system_title**: Environmental Management System
- **system_desc**: We have established an environmental management system that meets global standards based on ISO 14001 certification. We perform continuous improvement activities through the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle.
- **system_items**
  - Acquisition and maintenance of ISO 14001 certification
  - Compliance with environmental laws and regulations
- **ghg_title**: GHG Reduction
- **ghg_desc**: We systematically manage greenhouse gas emissions with the goal of achieving carbon neutrality by 2050. We are minimizing carbon emissions through the introduction of high-efficiency facilities and process improvements.
- **ghg_items**
  - Monitoring Scope 1, 2 emissions
  - Expanding use of renewable energy
- **resource_title**: Resource Circulation
- **resource_desc**: We contribute to the realization of a circular economy by suppressing waste generation and increasing recycling rates. We are strengthening water resource management and hazardous chemical management processes.
- **resource_items**
  - Improving waste recycling rate
  - Water consumption reduction activities
- **policy_title**: Environmental Management Policy
- **policy_desc**: A-PRO recognizes environmental preservation as a major element of corporate management and strives to minimize environmental impact throughout the entire process from product development, production, to disposal. All employees are familiar with and practice the environmental policy.
- **toxic_title**: Hazardous Substance Management
- **toxic_desc**: We comply with international environmental regulations such as RoHS and REACH, and strictly limit the use of hazardous substances in products. We guarantee the non-use of hazardous substances through a supply chain management system with partners.
- **safety_title**: Safety and Health Management
- **safety_desc**: As a leading company in the secondary battery equipment industry, A-PRO pursues safety, health, and happiness as absolute values.
- **safety_policy_title**: Safety and Health Policy
- **safety_items**
  - Strict compliance with industrial safety laws and prior prevention of risks
  - Cultivating safety awareness through voluntary education and practice of all members
  - Prevention of industrial accidents by complying with safety/health plans and manuals
  - Maintaining the best safety and health status with sufficient manpower and budget allocation
  - Practicing ESG policies for communication and mutual growth with partners
- **policy_btn**: A-PRO Environmental Policy
