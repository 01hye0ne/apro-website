Notice of Small-Scale Merger

Pursuant to Article 527-3 of the Commercial Act, we hereby give public notice that we have entered into a small-scale merger agreement as described below, with the approval of the Board of Directors serving in lieu of approval from a General Meeting of Shareholders.

– Details –

1. Method of Merger:
APRO Co., Ltd. shall absorb and merge APRO Partners Co., Ltd. and one other company.

2. Merger Ratio:
Surviving Company (1.0000000) : Disappearing Company (0.0000000)

3. Companies Involved in Merger:

  • Surviving Company: APRO Co., Ltd.

  • Disappearing Companies:

    1. APRO Partners Co., Ltd.

    2. Ecovolt Co., Ltd.

4. Date of Merger: December 02, 2024

5. Process:
Pursuant to Article 527-3 of the Commercial Act, the merger between the Surviving Company and the Disappearing Companies shall be conducted via a resolution of the Board of Directors without obtaining approval from a General Meeting of Shareholders.

6. Guidance on Exercise of Dissenting Opinions:

1) Procedure:
Shareholders who oppose the Board of Directors' resolution regarding the small-scale merger (limited to shareholders listed in the shareholder register of APRO Co., Ltd. as of October 11, 2024) must complete the attached "Notice of Dissent to Board Resolution" within two weeks from the date of this notice (October 11, 2024 – October 25, 2024).

  • Shareholders of Record (Direct Shareholders): Please send the notice to the Company by October 25, 2024.

  • Beneficial Owners (Shareholders via Securities Companies): Please submit the notice to your respective securities company by October 25, 2024 (three business days prior to the deadline for the notice of dissent).

  • Company Address: Attn: IR Manager, Management Planning Team, APRO Global Center, 41-14, Burim-ro 170beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang-si, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea

  • Phone Number: 031-689-3963

2) Appraisal Rights (Stock Purchase Rights):
Pursuant to Article 527-3 of the Commercial Act, appraisal rights are not recognized/granted.

3) Requirement for General Meeting:
Pursuant to Article 527-3 of the Commercial Act, if shareholders holding 20 percent or more of the total issued shares notify the Company of their dissent regarding the small-scale merger, the Company must obtain approval from a General Meeting of Shareholders.
